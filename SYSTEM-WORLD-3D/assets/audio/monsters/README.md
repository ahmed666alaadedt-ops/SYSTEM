
# SYSTEM WORLD - Monsters Audio Library

Original monster sound library for historical, fantasy, modern and Greed Island environments.

## Monster Sounds
- Deep roars and growls.
- Breathing and heavy breathing.
- Warning and intimidation sounds.
- Movement and footsteps.
- Wing flapping.
- Claws and body movement.
- Attack and special attack sounds.
- Pain and injury reactions.
- Death and defeat sounds.

## Boss Sounds
- Unique boss identity sounds.
- Massive roars.
- Boss awakening.
- Phase transition sounds.
- Enraged attack sounds.
- Environmental destruction.
- Defeat and victory sequences.

## Dynamic Audio
- Different sounds for every monster species.
- Sound intensity changes with monster behavior.
- 3D positional audio and distance attenuation.
- Boss sounds should feel powerful and cinematic.
- Limit simultaneous sounds for mobile performance.

## Technical Standards
- OGG Vorbis preferred.
- Android optimized.
- Original recordings or properly licensed assets only.
