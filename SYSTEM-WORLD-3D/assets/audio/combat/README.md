
# SYSTEM WORLD - Combat Audio Library

Cinematic combat audio system for all four worlds.

## Combat Start
- Heavy war drums.
- Deep cinematic impacts.
- Rising tension.
- Combat initiation effects.

## Fighting Sounds
- Punches and kicks.
- Sword clashes.
- Weapon impacts.
- Fast movement and dodging.
- Air rushes and powerful strikes.
- Explosions and ability effects.
- Armor and equipment movement.

## Boss Battles
- Epic orchestral music.
- Deep drums and cinematic choir.
- Unique boss entrance.
- Boss attack and phase transitions.
- Increasing intensity during dangerous phases.

## Combat Results
- Victory impact and achievement music.
- Defeat music.
- Sudden music transition after battle.
- Reward and loot sounds.

## Dynamic Music
- Music intensity responds to combat events.
- Different musical identity for each world.
- Smooth transitions between exploration and combat.
- Avoid repetitive loops.

## Technical Standards
- OGG Vorbis preferred.
- Android optimized.
- Original compositions or properly licensed recordings.
