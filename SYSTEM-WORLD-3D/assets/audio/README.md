
# SYSTEM WORLD - Audio Library

Central audio library for all four SYSTEM WORLD environments.

## Folder Structure

- ambient/ - Cities, forests, deserts, beaches, rivers.
- weather/ - Rain, thunder, wind and storms.
- animals/ - Animal sounds and wildlife.
- animals/birds/ - Bird calls and wing sounds.
- monsters/ - Monster voices, attacks and reactions.
- combat/ - Fighting, weapons, bosses and victory sounds.
- voices/ - Player and NPC voices.
- ui/ - Buttons, menus and notifications.

## Audio Standards

- Preferred format: OGG Vorbis.
- Use optimized audio files for Android.
- Keep environmental sounds separate from music.
- Use original or properly licensed recordings.
- Avoid copyrighted music and sound effects without permission.

## Important

Audio files are added separately.
The audio manager references their expected paths.
