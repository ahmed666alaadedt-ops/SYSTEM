
# SYSTEM WORLD - Ambient Audio

Environmental sound library for all four worlds.

## Sound Categories

### Modern World
- City traffic and street life.
- Shops, markets and public places.
- Modern buildings and indoor environments.

### Historical World
- Ancient cities and marketplaces.
- Traditional workshops and villages.
- Temples, palaces and historical environments.

### Fantasy World
- Magical forests and mysterious caves.
- Ancient ruins and supernatural locations.
- Fantasy villages and enchanted environments.

### Greed Island
- Floating island atmosphere.
- Strange forests and unexplored regions.
- Unique seasonal environmental sounds.

### Nature
- Forests, deserts and mountains.
- Beaches, oceans, rivers and waterfalls.
- Day, night and seasonal ambience.

## Audio Format
OGG Vorbis, optimized for Android.
