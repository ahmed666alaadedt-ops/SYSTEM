
# SYSTEM WORLD - Character and NPC Voice Library

Voice audio system for players, NPCs and AI-driven characters across all four worlds.

## Player Voice
- Breathing and heavy breathing.
- Running and exertion.
- Pain and injury reactions.
- Jumping and falling.
- Combat shouts.
- Victory and defeat reactions.
- Emotional expressions.

## NPC Voices
- Greetings and conversations.
- Different voices and personalities.
- Laughing and crying.
- Fear, anger and surprise.
- Trading and working conversations.
- Family and social interactions.
- Walking and activity reactions.

## Dialogue System
- Support for multiple languages.
- Voice lines connected to dialogue events.
- Optional subtitles.
- Different voice identities for characters.
- Context-aware conversations.

## World Identity
- Modern everyday speech.
- Historical languages and cultural atmosphere.
- Fantasy character voices.
- Unique Greed Island characters.

## Audio Behavior
- Voices should match character identity.
- Spatial 3D positioning.
- Distance-based volume.
- Avoid overlapping too many voices.
- Support voice and subtitle settings.

## Technical Standards
- OGG Vorbis preferred.
- Android optimized.
- Original or properly licensed recordings only.
