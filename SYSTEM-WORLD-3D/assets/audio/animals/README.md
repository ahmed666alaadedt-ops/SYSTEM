
# SYSTEM WORLD - Animals and Birds Audio

Wildlife and animal sound library for all four worlds.

## Birds
- Small birds singing.
- Crows and ravens.
- Eagles and falcons.
- Pigeons.
- Seagulls.
- Wing flapping and bird movement.
- Different calls for morning, daytime and night.

## Domestic Animals
- Dogs and puppies.
- Cats.
- Horses and foals.
- Cows and calves.
- Sheep and goats.
- Chickens and roosters.

## Wild Animals
- Wolves.
- Lions.
- Bears.
- Foxes.
- Deer.
- Elephants.
- Snakes and other wildlife.

## Audio Behavior
- Sounds should match the animal species.
- Different calls for movement, fear, pain and communication.
- 3D positional audio based on distance and direction.
- Avoid playing too many animal sounds simultaneously.
- Each world may have its own wildlife.

## Technical Standards
- Preferred format: OGG Vorbis.
- Optimized for Android.
- Use original or properly licensed recordings.
